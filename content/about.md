# The blog

Software Pendula is a notebook on software architecture and, more and more, on AI. Mostly I write about things I have had to think through properly, either as part of my work or sometimes just out of interest. I find writing down my thoughts helps me clarify and internalise them. Coming back to what I've written years later also serves to remind me how wrong I can be sometimes despite having been so confident at the time. It's a valuable life lesson. :smile:

The name comes from an old observation: trends in software development swing from one extreme to the other and then settle somewhere in between, like a pendulum. Static and dynamic languages are one example, and XML configuration and annotations another. My [first post](/post/software-pendula/) has the full, slightly corny explanation, along with how embarrassingly optimistic I was about how often I would post.

# Projects

- **[ai-soccer](https://github.com/eparkinson/ai-soccer)** is a small physics-based soccer game for teams controlled by code, which I use as a test bed for learning algorithms. [Part 1](/post/ai-soccer-part-1/) describes the game and [part 2](/post/ai-soccer-part-2/) covers what happened when the players had to learn it themselves, and what that cost.
- **This site** started out on Google's Blogger following a (rare) New Year's resolution in 2013. Since 2021 it has been a static site built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and served from AWS, mostly as a way to learn more about the tech. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/eparkinson/softwarependula).

# The author

My name is Elroy Parkinson. I live in Johannesburg, South Africa, and I am a solutions architect with more than two decades in software. I love finding elegant, simple, cost-effective technical solutions to tough business problems. I am particularly interested in cloud architecture, Kubernetes, and how AI is changing the way software gets built.

I have a Masters Degree in Computer Science from the University of Port Elizabeth (now [Nelson Mandela University](https://www.mandela.ac.za/)) and an MBA from [Charles Sturt University](https://www.csu.edu.au/) in Australia.
